Put your Vectrex cartridge ROMs here (.bin, .vec, .gam, .rom). Subfolders are fine: the ROM browser
shows them as folders.
The ROM browser (click the cartridge below the picture, or press L) lists them.
Without a cartridge the Vectrex plays its built-in game, Mine Storm.

Ultimate Vectrex Multicart 2 (UVMC2) games (.um2) go here too - the emulator runs them on its own UVMC2,
plug and play. "UVMC2 Demo.um2" is a small demo that shows it working: a rotating 3D cube computed on the
cartridge's RP2350 chip. Some UVMC2 games read data from the cartridge's SD card: that is the folder sd
next to the program (see the README in it).
"UVMC2 Movie Player.um2" plays Vectrex vector movies (the PiTrex / VecFever ones, such as Bad Apple) from the SD
card folder movies (sd/movies here) - the movies themselves are not included.
Get them from Malban's free PiTrex SD card download (vide.malban.de): copy its vectrex/movies folder into
sd/movies.
